It's the Small Things

It's not one big thing about you. It's a hundred tiny ones. The way you laugh before the punchline because you can't hold it in. The way you get when you talk about something you love — like the whole world narrows down to that one thing. The way you notice people. Really notice them.

I could list a thousand of these, and it still wouldn't be the whole story. But here's the truth underneath all of them: being around you feels like exhaling.

What You Don't See

You have this habit of doubting yourself — of talking about the things you wish you were instead of seeing what you already are. So let me be the one to say it, plainly, just this once:

You are kind in a way that costs you something. You are braver than you give yourself credit for. And you matter to more people than you'll ever know — including me, more than you know.

The Honest Part

Here's the part that's hard to say out loud. Somewhere along the way, my thoughts started saving a seat for you. A song comes on and I wonder if you'd like it. Something funny happens and you're the first person I want to tell. I'll catch myself smiling at my phone over a message from you, and think — oh. Oh no. This is real.

What I'm Not Asking

I want to be clear about something, because it matters: I'm not asking you to feel the same way. I'm not trying to rush anything, or make things weird, or turn a good thing into a complicated one. What we have is already worth a lot to me, and I don't want to lose it.

I just believe you deserve to know that someone sees you this way. That's all this is. No pressure. No expectations. Just the truth, finally out of my chest.

If You Feel Even a Little…

But if — and only if — some small part of you has ever wondered too… then maybe, just maybe, we could find out what this is. Slowly. No scripts, no pressure. A walk. A coffee. A terrible movie we both pretend to hate. I'd love any of it, as long as it's with you.
